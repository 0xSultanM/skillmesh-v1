Privacy

SkillMesh is built so that we never need to keep your data.

What happens to your resume

  • Your file is read in memory on our server to extract its text, then discarded. It is never written to disk or to a database.
  • If AI extraction is enabled, the text is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) for analysis. Before sending, we remove email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses and social profile links.
  • We don't log request contents.

Job postings

  • Job URLs are fetched once to read the posting. We only read public pages, never pages that need a login.
  • Screenshots are read in your browser using on-device OCR. The image is never uploaded.

Your history

Saved analyses and roadmap progress live only in this browser (local storage). You can delete them at any time with “Delete all local data” in the History panel.

What we do store

Only public data: our skill taxonomy and a cache of free learning resources (tutorials, playlists, docs) that we recommend.
